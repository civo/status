2026-08-13 17:08 UTC

Our data centre partner has confirmed the cooling issues were caused by multiple utility power disturbances resulting from overnight storms in the Phoenix area. One of the facility's chillers has been restored to operation and space temperatures have reduced significantly, with further improvement expected. Work continues on restoring the remaining cooling capacity, including additional temporary cooling and backup power equipment being brought on site over the coming hours.

API service to the PHX1 region has recovered. However, until cooling is fully restored we cannot rule out further periods of instability, and customers may still experience intermittent issues with API calls or workloads in this region. We are keeping this incident open and monitoring closely, and will post further updates as the remediation work progresses.
